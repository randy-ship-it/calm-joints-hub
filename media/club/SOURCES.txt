Club partner CTA visuals (illustrative mockups)

club-qr-<sport>.jpg (stand-up sign) and club-qr-<sport>-wall.jpg (wall, glass or fence mount)
for squash, tennis and pickleball, plus -640.webp / -1200.webp variants of each.
  Scenes: original AI-generated photographic renders made for Calm Joints on Nov 10, 2026
  (no people, no third-party photos, no attribution required). Each render had a blank sign panel.
  Sign faces: designed in HTML (Calm Joints wordmark, a "Your Club" logo placeholder, and a real QR code),
  then perspective-warped and light-matched onto the blank panel.
  Every QR was decoded with OpenCV after compositing and opens https://calmjoints.org/squash, /tennis or /pickleball.
  Placements: a 24 x 36 in portrait A-frame sign at court side, and a compact co-branded sign mounted on the
  court glass (squash), the court fence (tennis) or the side wall (pickleball).

club-store.jpg

  scalehealth.ca itself was not edited.
