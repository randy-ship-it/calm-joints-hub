# Racquets: five court sports | Calm Joints

> Squash, tennis, pickleball, padel and badminton. Each page has a short kit for between matches, and a video assessment with a registered physiotherapist.

## Pick a court
- Squash, stay on court longer: https://calmjoints.org/squash (Markdown: https://calmjoints.org/squash.md)
- Tennis, stay in the rally: https://calmjoints.org/tennis (Markdown: https://calmjoints.org/tennis.md)
- Pickleball, stay in the point: https://calmjoints.org/pickleball (Markdown: https://calmjoints.org/pickleball.md)
- Padel, stay on the glass: https://calmjoints.org/paddle
- Badminton, stay quick at the net: https://calmjoints.org/badminton

## How to book an assessment
1. Go to https://calmjoints.org/?intent=book
2. Pick a video visit time.
3. Meet a registered physiotherapist on video. They look at how you move and talk through next steps.

## Clubs
Private athletic and racquet clubs: Tier 1 co-branded physio QR (25% payout on net booking revenue to the club) or Tier 2 co-branded member store at wholesale rates. Request at https://calmjoints.org/squash#club-partner, https://calmjoints.org/tennis#club-partner or https://calmjoints.org/pickleball#club-partner.

Products open in the Industry Insider's Store: https://www.scalehealth.ca/calmjoints?src=cjsite&place=home. Not emergency care; call 911 in an emergency.
