# Partner with Calm Joints | Calm Joints

> Physio care for your members, customers or team. Video visits with registered physiotherapists. Nothing to manage on your side. No cost to get started.

## Who it is for
- Venues and sports facilities: clubs, courts, arenas and courses.
- Health services companies: clinics and health teams that want more reach.
- Fitness and wellness studios and clubs: Pilates, yoga, strength and members' clubs.
- Health and wellness creators.

## How to start
1. Send the form at https://calmjoints.org/partners (business type, approximate members or customers, city).
2. A person on the team gets back to you.

## Doctor clinics
- Option 1, a spare room: registered physiotherapists placed in an empty room under a simple fair-market lease. Patients book at calmjoints.org.
- Option 2, a free educational waiting-room kit (acrylic stand, cards and a poster with a QR code). A $299 deposit covers production and shipping risk, refunded in full after a 6-month pilot if the materials stay in place. No payments, commissions or incentives tied to scans or bookings.

## Private athletic and racquet clubs
- Tier 1, physio only: display a co-branded QR code. The club earns a 25% payout on net revenue from bookings made through it.
- Tier 2, full recovery activation: a co-branded member store at wholesale rates, with physiotherapy built in.
- Request either tier at https://calmjoints.org/squash#club-partner, https://calmjoints.org/tennis#club-partner or https://calmjoints.org/pickleball#club-partner.
