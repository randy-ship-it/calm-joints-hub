# Managed Physio. One click to physio. | Calm Joints

> Scale runs online physio for your customers or employees. They book on the Calm Joints site. Fully managed by Scale. Your business shares in the revenue. Canada-wide now. USA planned Q1 2027.

## Who it is for
Multi-location wellness, health apps, retirement homes, hospitals and employee benefits.

## How it works
1. Customers or employees book a video visit on the Calm Joints site.
2. Scale runs the digital physio visit.
3. In person only if needed. The store perk is optional.

## Options
- Option 1, Managed Physio: Scale runs the visits for your customers or employees. They book on the Calm Joints site. The setup is agreed with you.
- Option 2, Managed Physio plus a turnkey store: everything in option 1, plus a co-branded store loaded with products and services. 35% of the net revenue Scale collects on products and services (not physio bookings, and not gross sales). Employers can plug staff into that physio and offer insider pricing on eligible products as a workplace perk.

## How to start
1. Fill in the form at https://calmjoints.org/enterprise#talk (company, work email, organization type, users or members).
2. The team replies within one business day.
3. Setup is agreed with you. Booking stays on the Calm Joints site.

The scenario calculator on the page (https://calmjoints.org/enterprise#estimate) is illustrative only. It is not a guaranteed payout.
