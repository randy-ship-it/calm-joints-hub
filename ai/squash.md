# Squash: Stay on court longer. | Calm Joints

> Squash puts real load on ankles, knees and wrists: hard lunges, fast turns, back-to-back matches. Calm Joints offers a short kit for between matches and a video assessment with a registered physiotherapist.

## Who it is for
Squash players who want to stay on court, and private clubs that want physio for their members.

## Book a video assessment
1. Go to https://calmjoints.org/?intent=book (or "Book a Calm Joints assessment" on https://calmjoints.org/squash).
2. Pick a video visit that fits around your matches.
3. Meet a registered physiotherapist on video from home. They look at how you move and talk through next steps with you.

## A short kit for between matches
Opens in the Industry Insider's Store (https://www.scalehealth.ca/calmjoints?src=cjsite&place=home). Prices and availability are on the linked pages.
- TENS Pro Essentials (DR-HO'S): one option between matches, not something every player needs. Follow the maker's directions.
- R8 Deep Tissue Massage Tool (ROLL Recovery): quads, calves and hamstrings.
- R3 Orthopedic Foot Roller (ROLL Recovery): a compact foot roller for after court time.
- StretchMat (ROLL Recovery): a marked mat for a post-matche stretch.

## For private clubs
- Tier 1, physio only: display a co-branded QR code. Anyone who books through it gets sessions with registered therapists across the province, and the club earns a 25% payout on net revenue from those bookings.
- Tier 2, full recovery activation: a co-branded member store at wholesale rates, with physiotherapy built in.
- Hands-off for club staff. Request either tier at https://calmjoints.org/squash#club-partner. The calculator at https://calmjoints.org/enterprise#estimate is illustrative only, not a payout guarantee.

## FAQ
Q: Where can I see a physiotherapist online for a squash injury in Canada?
A: Calm Joints offers video assessments with registered physiotherapists. Book at https://calmjoints.org/?intent=book

Q: Is this emergency care?
