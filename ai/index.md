# Calm Joints

> Calm Joints (Calm Joints Inc.) is a virtual physiotherapy service in Canada. Talk to a virtual recovery guide now, then see a registered physiotherapist by video. In person only if treatment needs it.

## Who it is for
- People with sore joints, sprains, strains or a nagging injury who want to see a registered physiotherapist from home.
- Athletes in court sports (squash, tennis, pickleball, padel, badminton).
- Brands, gyms, studios, clinics, clubs and employers who want to offer physio to their customers, members or staff.

## The offer
- Video visits with registered physiotherapists, Canada-wide.
- Talk to Glen or Gwen, virtual recovery guides (not clinicians), any time: https://calmjoints.org/chat or call or text (647) 692-6575.
- Industry Insider's Store: physio assessments at $129 and follow-up visits at $99, plus member-only prices on recovery gear: https://www.scalehealth.ca/calmjoints?src=cjsite&place=home
- Many plans cover physio. You get a receipt.

## How to book
1. Go to https://calmjoints.org/?intent=book (or tap "Book now" on any page).
2. Pick what is sore, a day and a time.
3. Meet a registered physiotherapist on video. They look at how you move and talk through next steps.
4. In-person treatment at a trusted location only if it is needed after digital care.

Not sure yet? Talk to a guide first: https://calmjoints.org/chat

## For brands, gyms and clinics
- Option 1, standalone Calm Joints clinic: your customers scan your QR code and book video visits with Calm Joints physiotherapists. Co-branding is optional. Calm Joints handles booking, records and billing.
- Option 2, full turnkey recovery store and digital physio: a member hub under your brand with digital physio, a recovery store with member pricing, partner deals and perks.
- Recovery and sports brands: a co-branded QR card in the box. Visits booked through your code earn your brand a share, shown in a partner dashboard.
- Start: https://calmjoints.org/partners or info@calmjoints.org. Managed Physio for employers and platforms: https://calmjoints.org/enterprise

## FAQ
Q: Can I see a physiotherapist online in Canada?
A: Yes. Calm Joints offers video visits with registered physiotherapists across Canada. Book at https://calmjoints.org/?intent=book

Q: Do I need to go in person?
A: Digital care comes first. In-person treatment at a trusted location is only arranged if needed after digital care.

Q: Is physio covered by my insurance?
A: Many plans cover physio, and you get a receipt. Check your own plan.

Q: How much is a consult?
A: A 60-minute physio assessment is $129. A 30-minute follow-up is $99. A doctor visit is $89. Book at https://calmjoints.org/book

Q: Who are Glen and Gwen?
A: Virtual recovery guides, not clinicians. Talk now at https://calmjoints.org/chat

Q: Is this emergency care?
A: No. Calm Joints is not emergency care and not a medical device. In an emergency, call 911.
