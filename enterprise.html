FAQ

Enterprise questions, answered.

What is the Calm Joints Physio API?

It is a way for platforms and businesses to offer instant physiotherapy booking with registered physiotherapists across Canada. Partners connect by API, by an embeddable booking widget, or with a co-branded physio hub that Calm Joints builds and runs.

Who is it for?

Digital health apps, e-commerce and retail websites, retirement and senior living communities, hospitals and health systems, employers and insurers or benefits providers that want to offer physio to their users, residents, patients or members.

How much work is it for our team?

Very little. Co-branded partnerships are 100% turnkey and done for you: we handle the providers, scheduling, booking flow, receipts, support and reporting. API partners can go deeper if they want to.

Is care available 24/7?

Virtual care is available 24/7 through Calm Joints. Booked appointments are with registered physiotherapists at the times shown in their real calendars.

Where is Calm Joints available?

Across Canada. Calm Joints is a virtual service with more than 200 trusted providers in our network across the country.

What is the turnkey store?

An optional, fully stocked recovery store under your brand or co-branded with Calm Joints. We source the products, run fulfilment and handle customer service.

Is Calm Joints a medical device or a replacement for emergency care?

No. Calm Joints connects people with registered physiotherapists. It is not a medical device and is not emergency care. In an emergency, call 911.
