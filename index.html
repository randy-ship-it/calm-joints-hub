Questions, answered

Is Calm Joints its own clinic?

No. Calm Joints is a CHI trade-name storefront / Scale clinic hub. Clinical physiotherapy is delivered by Align physiotherapists on the Scale network.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book a virtual physiotherapy assessment directly.

Is this covered by insurance?

Depending on your plan, some or all of the physio fee may be covered. Align provides insurance-friendly receipts for eligible services — confirm with your benefits provider.

What is Scale Health?

Scale Health is the virtual clinic and wellness membership network behind this hub. Membership is free. Clinical appointments are booked separately with Align physios.
