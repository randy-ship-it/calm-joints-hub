Questions, answered plainly

Is Calm Joints its own clinic?

Calm Joints is a trade name of Clairvoyant Holdings Inc. (CHI). Clinical physiotherapy is delivered by Align physiotherapists. Scale Health powers the hub. Write info@calmjoints.ca if you need the long version.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book a virtual physiotherapy assessment directly.

Will insurance care?

Some or all of the physio fee may be covered, depending on your plan. Align provides insurance-friendly receipts for eligible services. Your benefits provider has the final say.

I’m a physio. Can I join from any province?

Yes. The hello form covers every province and territory. Registration number is optional on the form — bring the details you’re comfortable sharing, and we’ll take it from there.

How do I reach a human?

Email info@calmjoints.ca. That’s the inbox for members, physios, and “wait, what is this?”
