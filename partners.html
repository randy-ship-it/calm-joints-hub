Proposed partner terms

Proposed: 25% of net billing on patients you send.

Net billing is what the clinic bills for a visit, minus what we pay the physiotherapist who gave it. Under the proposed terms, you’d get a quarter of what’s left each month, for as long as your people keep coming.

You don’t hire anyone, run anything, or touch patient records. We do the care, the booking and the billing.

Proposed, not final. These terms aren’t a live offer yet. The partner agreement and its tax treatment, including HST, are under legal review. We’ll confirm final terms in writing before anyone signs or is paid.

Partner basics

The short version

What do I actually do?

Put your signs up and keep them visible. That’s it. You don’t run a clinic, hire staff or handle any health information.

Who provides the care?

Calm Joints. Every visit is with a registered physiotherapist, and we handle booking, records and billing.

How do you know who came from me?

Your QR code is only yours. Every scan is tagged to your code, and people can leave an email before they book so we can match their visits to you.

When do I get paid?

Under the proposed terms, monthly, with a statement showing visits from your code and your share. The terms are being finalized, and we’ll sign the partner agreement with you before any payout.

Can I stop any time?

Yes. Take your signs down and email info@calmjoints.org.

Anything I can’t say on my signs?

Please keep the kit as it is. Don’t add health claims or promises about results.
