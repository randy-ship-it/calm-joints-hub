Physio for your patients, with nothing to manage.

Calm Joints is a digital physiotherapy service with registered physiotherapists. Patients book at calmjoints.org. If either option below is useful, it takes very little on your side.

Option 1

A spare room

If you have a room that sits empty, we can place registered physiotherapists there under a simple fair-market lease. Patients book at calmjoints.org.

Option 2

A free educational waiting-room kit

A small acrylic stand, cards and a poster with a QR code to calmjoints.org. Patients can learn about virtual physiotherapy, or book from their phone while they wait.

Kit terms, in plain language

  • The kit is free. We ask for a $299 deposit to cover our production and shipping risk.

  • There are no payments, commissions or incentives tied to scans or bookings, and no other fees.
  • The materials are educational only. They are not medical advice and do not replace your care.
