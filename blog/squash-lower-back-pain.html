Lower back pain after squash: what helps, and when it is urgent

Calm Joints · November 8, 2026 · About 5 min read

Squash has you bending, reaching, and rotating. Sports Medicine Australia includes the lower back among the usual sites for strains and sprains in the sport.[1] Most of that pain is a muscle or joint irritation rather than a dangerous injury, and the NHS advises against staying in bed for long periods.[2]

Why a match can stir it up

  • A long match, a tournament weekend, or a sudden jump in court time.
  • Low balls reached by bending from the back instead of the legs.
  • Going from a desk or a car straight into hard rallies.

Those are common stories. They are not a diagnosis.

What usually helps

Keep moving. The NHS says not to stay in bed for long periods, and that ice or heat and simple movement can ease stiffness.[2] NICE includes exercise, matched to the person, in the care offered for low back pain.[3][4] Easing off the longest matches for a few days is reasonable. A list of stretches with set counts is not, until someone has assessed you.

NICE says not to offer belts or corsets for managing low back pain.[3]

Call 911 or go to emergency care if back pain comes with:[2]
  • Pain, tingling, weakness, or numbness in both legs.
  • Loss of feeling around the genitals or anus.
  • A change in bladder or bowel control, including trouble peeing or leaking.
  • A change in sexual sensation, or trouble getting or keeping an erection.
The NHS lists these as reasons to call emergency services. Do not wait for a physio appointment. In Canada, call 911.

Frequently asked questions

Should I rest in bed after squash back pain?

The NHS says not to stay in bed for long periods. Gentle activity is the usual advice for ordinary low back pain.[2]

Should I wear a back brace to play?

NICE says not to offer belts or corsets for managing low back pain.[3] A brace is not the long-term answer in that guidance.

Back keeps flaring after squash?

A virtual visit can look at how you move and what to change on court. This article is not that visit.
